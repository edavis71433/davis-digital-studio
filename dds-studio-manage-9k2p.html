💡 How to use these
Find a business, log it in Prospects, run its site through your AI Review tool, then paste a template into your sending tool and fill in the brackets. The personalized hook converts better because it references something real about their actual site. Keep it to 15 to 20 a day from your domain so your sender reputation stays healthy, and let your warm-up run for about two weeks before the first real send.
