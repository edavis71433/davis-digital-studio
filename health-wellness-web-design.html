The problem

Why businesses like yours struggle to grow online

Wellness clients need to trust you before they book. If your website doesn't build that trust quickly, they move on to someone whose does.

No clear first step for new clients

Someone visiting for the first time is anxious about getting started. Your website needs to answer "what do I do first?" with a simple, obvious next step on every page.

Practitioner not introduced properly

People choose wellness providers based on personal fit and trust. Bios, credentials, photos, and your approach need prominent placement, not buried in a tiny About page.

Not showing up for local searches

"Therapist near me," "yoga studio [city]," "personal trainer [neighborhood]", these are high-intent searches. If you're not in the top 3, those clients go to someone who is.

Scheduling friction

A contact form is not a booking system. Every extra step between "I'm interested" and "I have an appointment" loses clients to practices with frictionless online scheduling.

When someone searches for what you offer, your website should be the first thing they see.

What I deliver

Everything you actually need

A site that builds trust before the first appointment, makes scheduling effortless, and brings new clients through Google every week.

Online scheduling integration

Acuity, Jane, Mindbody, or SimplePractice booking built into every page. New client intake forms, appointment types, and cancellation policies handled cleanly.

Practitioner bios & credentials

Detailed bios with photos, credentials, specialties, and your approach to treatment. Trust is built before they walk in, and a strong bio is also a local SEO asset.

Services & pricing page

Clear, readable descriptions of each service, session length, and pricing. Transparency reduces the friction of the first call and attracts better-fit clients from the start.

Local SEO for wellness searches

On-page optimization targeting your specific modalities, location, and client type. Google Business Profile setup with photos, Q&A, and a review response strategy.

Client testimonials

Social proof from existing clients structured with schema markup so Google shows your star rating in search results before anyone even clicks your link.

New client resources & FAQs

What to expect on your first visit, what to bring, insurance questions, all answered on the site. Reduces pre-appointment anxiety and the volume of "just checking" phone calls.

Missing opportunities

Revenue you might be leaving on the table

Most wellness sites stop at a bio and a contact form. If you want more than a brochure, these are the kinds of custom systems I can build for studios, coaches, and wellness practitioners. They go beyond a standard website, and they're priced as a Custom Platform build.

Online session & class booking

Let clients book sessions or classes directly on your site, any time, with a deposit or prepayment up front to lock in the slot and cut no-shows.

Prepaid session packages & memberships

Sell multi-session packages and memberships through your site with Stripe checkout. Clients commit in advance and you get predictable, recurring revenue.

Intake & waiver forms

Custom intake questionnaires and liability waivers clients complete before their first visit, so you're prepared and they're ready to go on day one.

Note: these are general booking and intake tools for wellness businesses. For clinical or medical practices that handle protected health records, you'll want a HIPAA-compliant platform built for that purpose.
