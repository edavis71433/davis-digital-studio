How this is calculated

  • Online revenue = monthly revenue × % from online sources
  • Monthly gain = online revenue × improvement percentage
  • Annual gain = monthly gain × 12 months
  • ROI = (annual gain − investment) ÷ investment × 100
  • Payback period = investment ÷ monthly gain
