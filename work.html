Bacchus Kitchen · Restaurant & wine · Launching Q3 2026

One hidden problem. Six revenue streams.

When Bacchus Kitchen came to me, they didn't have a design problem — they had a discoverability problem. Six revenue streams (dining, catering, events, wine club, private venue, retail wine), and only one was easy to find online. The fix wasn't a prettier homepage. It was a strategy that made every revenue stream findable, then a build that made each one effortless to act on.

Just as important: how it felt to work together. From day one, the owner could see exactly what we were doing and why — the plan, the progress, the decisions, all in one organized place. No chasing, no guesswork, no lost files.
