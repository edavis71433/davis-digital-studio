Website Redesign + SEO Strategy · Los Angeles, CA

Six revenue streams. Five of them invisible online.

Bacchus Kitchen has live jazz, a full wine bar, a $100/month Wine Club, catering, venue rental, and gift cards. Six revenue streams. When I audited the site, five of them were nearly invisible. The menu page URL was literally /copy-of-wine-club-1. a leftover Wix template slug. Hours said "midweek hours vary." The menu was unreadable photos of printed paper. The footer still said © 2024. Zero catering or venue leads were coming from the web.

68% of diners research online before choosing a restaurant. A first-time visitor to Bacchus couldn't tell in five seconds if it was a wine shop, fine dining, or a jazz lounge. Every day that didn't change, money was walking out the door.
