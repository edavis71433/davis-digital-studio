Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Effective June 20, 2026

Davis Digital Studio is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are actively working to increase the accessibility of this website in accordance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines.

Our Commitment

We believe the web should work for everyone. This site is built with the following accessibility practices in mind:

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper heading hierarchy
  • Keyboard navigation support across all interactive elements
  • Skip-to-main-content link on every page
  • Visible focus indicators for keyboard users
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios for body text (targeting 4.5:1 minimum)
  • ARIA labels on navigation landmarks and interactive components
  • Responsive design that works at up to 400% zoom without horizontal scrolling
  • No auto-playing audio or video
  • Cookie and consent controls that are keyboard-accessible

Known Limitations

We are a small, actively maintained site and are continuously improving. Current known gaps we are working to address include:

  • Some decorative images may be missing descriptive alt text
  • Third-party embeds (Calendly, Stripe checkout) are governed by their own accessibility practices

We conduct periodic accessibility reviews and aim to resolve reported issues within 5 business days.

Assistive Technology Support

This site has been designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen readers including NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Browser zoom up to 400%
  • High contrast mode on Windows and macOS

Request an Accommodation

If you need information from this website in a different format, or encounter a barrier to access, please contact us. We'll respond within 2 business days and work to provide the content or service you need through an accessible alternative.

Report an Accessibility Issue

If you find a page or feature that doesn't work with your assistive technology, please let us know:

  • What page or feature you were trying to use
  • What assistive technology you were using (e.g., screen reader name and version)
  • What happened and what you expected to happen

Contact us at eric@davisdigitalstudio.com and we'll prioritize a fix.

Standards Reference

This site targets conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

This statement applies to davisdigitalstudio.com and all pages operated by Davis Digital Studio.
