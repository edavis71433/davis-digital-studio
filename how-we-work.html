How we work

Hiring a web designer usually means crossed wires and lost files. It doesn't have to.

From our first call to long after launch, working with me is built to feel calm, clear, and completely organized. Here's exactly what to expect at every step — and why nothing slips through the cracks.

Most designers run your project out of their inbox. I run yours through a system I built specifically so you always know where things stand — the plan, the files, the messages, and the money, all in one organized place. You'll never chase me for an update, dig for a file, or wonder what you owe.

01

The first conversation

It starts with a real conversation, not a sales pitch. We talk about your business, your customers, and what's actually getting in the way. Sometimes the answer is a new website. Sometimes it isn't — and I'll tell you either way. You'll leave with more clarity than you came in with, whether or not we work together.

You feel: heard, not sold to
02

Getting started, the organized way

The moment we begin, everything about your project gets its own home — one private space, your own login, where the plan, the timeline, the files, the messages, and the money all live together. No "which email was that in?" No scattered docs. From day one, you can see exactly what we're doing and what I need from you.

You feel: in control, from the very first day
03

The build, with you in the loop

As your site comes together, you're never left guessing. You can see what's done, what's in progress, and what's coming next. When it's time for your input, you'll know exactly what to look at — and approve it in seconds, not in a tangle of email threads. Every comment, file, and decision stays in one thread you can always find.

You feel: looped in, never chasing
04

Launch, handled

Launch day isn't a scramble. By the time your site goes live, everything has already been reviewed, approved, and accounted for. What you owe and what you've paid has been clear the whole way — no surprise invoices, no awkward money conversations. You launch with confidence, knowing nothing was missed.

You feel: calm on a day that's usually stressful
05

After launch, still here

I don't disappear once the site is live. Your project space stays open — your files, your history, your point of contact, all still in one place. When you need an update, a question answered, or help growing, you're not starting over with a stranger. You're picking up with the person who already knows your business.

You feel: like you have a partner, not a one-time vendor
Why it's this organized

Because I built the systems that run it myself — the same care I put into your website, I put into the experience of working with me. It's custom-built, it runs every project I take on, and it gets a little better every time. You don't buy any of it. You simply get it, because you hired Davis Digital Studio.

And every project makes it sharper. I build the way the best software gets built — by solving real problems in real work, not in theory. Today, that means you get an experience most agencies can't match. Where it goes from here, we'll let the work decide.
