Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Effective June 20, 2026

Davis Digital Studio ("we," "us," or "our") operates davisdigitalstudio.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website or use our services. By using our site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact forms. your name, email address, business name, and message when you reach out through our contact or audit request forms
  • Report Card requests. your business name, type, city, website URL, and email address when you request a free Digital Report Card
  • Payment information. when you purchase a Digital Health Check, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card information.
  • Client portal. if you are a client, we store your name, email, and project information to manage your project through our portal

We also collect information automatically through cookies and analytics tools, including pages visited, time spent on pages, browser type, device type, and approximate location (city/region level). We only activate analytics cookies after you consent via our cookie banner.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide services you request
  • To deliver your Digital Report Card or audit results
  • To process payments and manage your project
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it
  • To send you project updates if you are a client (no marketing emails without your consent)

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics 4. tracks website usage and visitor behavior. Only activated with your consent. IP anonymization is enabled. Google Privacy Policy
  • Formspree. processes contact form and audit request submissions. Formspree Privacy Policy
  • Stripe. processes payments for the Digital Health Check. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Calendly. manages discovery call bookings. Calendly Privacy Policy
  • Supabase. stores client portal data securely with row-level security. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Netlify. hosts this website. Netlify Privacy Policy

Cookies

We use a cookie consent banner to request your permission before activating Google Analytics cookies. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or contacting us.

We do not use cookies for advertising or sell your data to third parties.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at eric@davisdigitalstudio.com.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and lead information for up to 2 years. Client project data is retained for the duration of the project and up to 1 year after completion. You may request deletion at any time.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption with HSTS, Content Security Policy headers, secure authentication for client portals with row-level security, and use of trusted third-party services with their own security practices. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Accessibility

We are committed to making this website accessible to all users. For information about our accessibility practices and to request accommodations, see our Accessibility Statement.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "last updated" date. Your continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
