The problem

Why businesses like yours stay invisible on Google

Most small businesses have never done real SEO. That means every day, customers who need exactly what you offer are finding your competitors instead.

You're invisible on Google

If you're not in the top 3 results for your main keywords, you're essentially invisible. 75% of users never scroll past the first page.

Your Google Business Profile is incomplete

GBP is the single highest-ROI move for any local business. Most profiles are missing photos, Q&A, services, and a review strategy, all of which affect rankings directly.

Your site has technical issues

Slow load times, missing meta tags, mobile unfriendliness, and broken structured data actively penalize your rankings. If the foundation is wrong, content can't save you.

No keyword strategy

Writing content without knowing what people actually search is guesswork. Real SEO starts with data: what your customers type, how often, how competitive it is, and what it would take to rank.

Real data, real rankings, real results, not vanity metrics.

What I deliver

Everything you actually need

A data-driven SEO strategy built for your specific business, market, and customers, not a generic checklist.

Keyword research with SEMrush

Data-driven keyword targeting based on what your actual customers search. Search volume, difficulty, intent, and opportunity, not guesswork.

Google Business Profile optimization

Complete GBP optimization with photos, hours, services, Q&A, and a post schedule. The single highest-ROI move for any local business.

On-page SEO implementation

Title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, internal linking, schema markup, and image alt tags, done correctly on every single page of your site.

Technical SEO audit & fixes

Site speed, mobile usability, crawlability, canonical tags, and structured data, the foundation Google needs to rank you. Fixed, not just reported.

Competitor analysis

What are your competitors ranking for that you're not? Where are the gaps you can fill? A clear picture of the competitive landscape and what it would take to beat them.

Monthly reporting

A plain-English report every month: what's ranking, what's improving, what we're focusing on next. No jargon, no vanity metrics, no hiding behind a dashboard.
