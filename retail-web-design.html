The problem

Why most retail & boutique websites lose customers

A weak website doesn't just look bad. It actively costs you leads, bookings, and revenue every single day.

Hours and location hard to find

A shopper who can't find your hours in 5 seconds will just order on Amazon. Hours, address, and a map need to be on every page.

No product preview online

78% of shoppers want to see what's in stock before visiting. A simple product catalog — even without e-commerce — drives real foot traffic.

Missing Google Business Profile

GBP is how your store shows up in 'near me' searches. Without one, you're invisible to the 3 billion Google searches that happen every day.

No story or brand identity

Boutiques win on experience and identity — not price. Your website needs to communicate what makes your store worth the visit before they leave the couch.

What I build

Everything your retail & boutiquebusiness actually needs

Not a template. A site built around how your specific customers find, evaluate, and decide to hire you.

Product catalog & online store

A browsable product catalog that showcases inventory and, if you're ready, an integrated online store. Even a catalog without checkout increases in-store visits.

Google Business Profile setup

Complete GBP optimization so you show in 'near me' searches and on Google Maps. Photos, hours, holiday hours, Q&A, and a review response process.

Brand story & About page

Boutiques beat big box stores on story and trust. An about page that communicates why you started the store and what makes it different converts better than any product photo.

Events & sale promotions

Trunk shows, pop-ups, seasonal sales, and loyalty events — all promotable through the website and a simple email list. Drives repeat visits from existing customers.

Gift card & wishlist integration

Gift cards are your highest-margin product and easiest upsell. Wishlist functionality keeps customers coming back and gives partners a gift-giving shortcut.

Local SEO for product searches

Targeting searches like '[product type] near me', '[your neighborhood] boutique', and seasonal terms. Your website becomes the thing that pulls people in off Google.
