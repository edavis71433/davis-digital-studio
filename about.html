About

I didn't learn business from a textbook. I learned it by starting at the bottom.

Call centers, reception desks, processing claims, talking to real people solving real problems. Nine roles over nearly a decade. That path taught me something no design course can: how a business actually works, and where it actually breaks.

I care more about outcomes than websites.

A lot of web designers start with colors, fonts, and layouts. I start with questions. Why aren't customers finding you? Why aren't visitors converting? Why are competitors showing up first? Sometimes the answer is a redesign. Sometimes it's SEO. Sometimes it's your messaging — sometimes it's something nobody has pointed out yet.

I've watched organizations spend enormous sums solving problems that better planning would have prevented. I've also seen small businesses get sold expensive sites that never brought in a single customer. My job isn't to sell you a website. It's to solve the problem in front of your business.

I'm also a first-generation college student finishing my M.S. in Business Analytics at CSUN as a Dean's Scholar. I started from the bottom of an org chart and worked up through nine roles to get here. That's not a sob story — it's a competitive advantage. I bring that same discipline to every client project.

Why everything runs so smoothly

The same discipline that carried me through nine roles is why I didn't just build websites — I built a studio that runs like a company. Every project moves through systems I designed so nothing gets lost and nothing gets forgotten. That organization isn't an accident. It's the whole point.

And it's something I'm still building. The best tools come from people who actually do the work; I'd rather prove something in real projects than promise it in a pitch.
