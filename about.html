I care more about
outcomes than websites.

A lot of web designers start with colors, fonts, and layouts. I start with questions. Why aren't customers finding you? Why aren't visitors converting? Why are competitors showing up ahead of you? What's actually stopping your business from growing?

Sometimes the answer is a redesign. Sometimes it's SEO. Sometimes it's your messaging. Sometimes it's something nobody has pointed out yet. My job isn't to sell you a website. My job is to help you make better business decisions online. Before launching Davis Digital Studio, I spent nearly a decade working inside a large enterprise digital ecosystem. My work has included enterprise website management, content operations, Adobe Experience Manager production, SEO-related initiatives, domain governance, vendor coordination, and large-scale digital programs. I've seen how large organizations think about structure, risk, search, governance, and user experience, and I started this studio because independent businesses deserve access to that same level of thinking without agency overhead or confusing jargon. That same background is why I can build more than websites. The client portal, admin panel, and tools that run this studio are all custom-built, and I bring that same capability to clients who need real platforms and web apps, not just pages.

I've watched organizations spend enormous amounts of money solving problems that could have been prevented with better planning. I've also seen small businesses get sold services they never needed. That's one of the reasons I started Davis Digital Studio. Independent businesses deserve strategic thinking without agency overhead, inflated retainers, or confusing jargon. You shouldn't have to spend $50,000 to get good advice.

I'm also a first-generation college student currently finishing my M.S. in Business Analytics at CSUN as a Dean's Scholar. I bring the same discipline to every client project.
