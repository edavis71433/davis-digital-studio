The experience

What it's actually like to work with me.

Hiring a web designer usually means crossed wires, lost files, and wondering what's happening. With me it feels different from the first day — calm, clear, and organized. Here's what to expect, from your side of the table.

01

You always know what's happening.

No wondering where your project stands or whether you've missed something. At any moment, you can see what's done, what's in progress, and what (if anything) I need from you. The guesswork that usually comes with hiring a designer just isn't there.

You feel: in control
02

Everything lives in one place.

Progress, files, approvals, invoices, and our conversation — all together in one private space that's yours. No digging through email threads, no "which version was that?", no scattered links. When you need something, you know exactly where it is.

You feel: organized
03

Reviewing and approving work is simple.

When something's ready for your eyes, you'll know — and you can look it over and give the go-ahead in a couple of clicks, on your own time. No long email chains trying to explain what changed. Just clear work, clearly presented, waiting for your okay.

You feel: looped in
04

Your files stay organized for you.

Every logo, document, and deliverable lives in one place, labeled and easy to find — not buried in an inbox or a download folder you'll lose track of. Months later, when you need that file again, it's still right there.

You feel: taken care of
05

Money is clear, never awkward.

What you owe and what you've already paid stays plain and easy to find the whole way through. No surprise invoices, no chasing, no uncomfortable money conversations. Just clarity, so the financial side never gets in the way of the work.

You feel: respected
06

Talking to me is easy — and it's actually me.

Our whole conversation stays in one place, so nothing gets lost and you're never re-explaining yourself. And when you reach out, you reach me — the person doing your work. No account managers, no junior handoff, no phone tree.

You feel: heard
07

After launch, I'm still here.

Launch day isn't goodbye. Your space stays open, your history stays intact, and I'm still your point of contact. When you need an update, a fix, or help getting found by more customers, you're picking up with someone who already knows your business — not starting over with a stranger.

You feel: like you have a partner
