The problem

Why most restaurant websites lose customers

A bad website doesn't just look unprofessional. it actively costs you covers, events, and private dining inquiries every day.

Not showing up on Google

If your restaurant doesn't appear in the top 3 for "[your cuisine] near me," someone else is getting those diners. Google Business Profile optimization and local SEO fix this.

Terrible mobile experience

Over 70% of restaurant searches happen on a phone. If your menu is a PDF, your hours are buried, or the site loads in 6 seconds. they're going to OpenTable or your competitor.

No clear path to book or order

A reservation button buried in the footer, no online ordering, no private dining inquiry form. Every friction point is a lost booking. Clear CTAs on every page fix this immediately.

Ignoring reviews and reputation

Your website should showcase your best reviews and link to your Google profile. A site that doesn't actively build social proof is leaving trust on the table.
