The problem

Why businesses like yours lose customers online

A slow, broken, or invisible website costs you covers, catering jobs, and event bookings every single day.

Not showing up on Google

If you're not in the top 3 results for "[your cuisine] near me," someone else is getting those diners. Google Business Profile optimization and local SEO fix this.

The menu is a photo or a PDF

Google can't read a photo of your menu. Your customers can't find dishes by searching. And a PDF is painful on mobile. A real HTML menu fixes all three.

No clear path to book or order

A reservation button buried in the footer, no private dining form, no catering inquiry page. Every friction point is a lost booking.

Five revenue streams invisible

Catering, venue rental, wine club, gift cards, events — if they're not on the website with their own pages, they don't exist online.

Great food deserves a website that shows it off — and gets it found.

What I deliver

Everything you actually need

Not a template. A site built around how your specific customers find, evaluate, and decide to visit or hire you.

Google Business Profile setup

Complete optimization with photos, hours, menu link, Q&A, and a review strategy. The single highest-ROI thing any restaurant can do.

Mobile-first menu

Fast-loading, readable HTML menu. No PDFs, no third-party embeds, no friction. Customers find what they want in two taps — and Google can index every dish.

Reservation & events integration

Tock, OpenTable, or direct booking forms. Private dining and catering inquiry pages that actually convert visitors into paying clients.

Revenue stream pages

Catering, venue rental, wine club, gift cards, live events — each with its own dedicated page, its own SEO, and its own lead capture.

Local SEO for "[cuisine] near me"

On-page optimization targeting the exact searches your future customers are making right now. Schema markup for menu, hours, location, and reviews.
