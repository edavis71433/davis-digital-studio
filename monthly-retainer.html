The problem

Why businesses like yours let their sites go stale

A site that was great at launch starts falling behind within months. Google notices. So do your customers.

Your site hasn't been updated in months

Stale content signals to Google, and to visitors, that nobody's home. Regular updates keep your site relevant, your rankings healthy, and your business looking active.

You don't have time to manage it

You're running a business. Updating copy, monitoring rankings, fixing broken links, and responding to Google's algorithm changes shouldn't be your job.

You don't know what's working

Without monthly reporting, you're flying blind. Which pages are getting traffic? Which keywords are moving? What should you focus on next? You shouldn't have to guess.

Your site is quietly falling behind

Competitors update their sites. Google's algorithm changes. Technology evolves. Without ongoing maintenance, your rankings and conversion rate slowly erode without you noticing.

Monthly reports in plain English, no dashboards, no jargon, just what matters.

What I deliver

Everything you need to stay ahead

Ongoing management that keeps your site fast, current, and moving up the rankings, so you can focus on running your business.

Content updates, fast

Menu changes, new staff, updated hours, seasonal promotions, submitted Monday, live by Wednesday. No login required on your end, no back-and-forth delays.

SEO monitoring & improvements

Rankings tracked monthly. If a keyword slips, I catch it and fix it. Ongoing on-page improvements to keep you moving up the search results.

Monthly performance report

Plain-English summary of traffic, rankings, and what we're focusing on next. Takes 5 minutes to read and actually tells you something useful about your business.

Technical health checks

Broken links, slow pages, and crawl errors caught before they become problems. Your site stays clean, fast, and indexed correctly, without you ever having to think about it.
