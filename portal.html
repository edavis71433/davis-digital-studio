📃 Davis Digital Studio — Project Agreement

1. Scope of Work
Davis Digital Studio ("Studio") agrees to provide web design, SEO strategy, and related digital services as described in the project proposal or scope of work. Any services not expressly included constitute out-of-scope work and will be quoted separately in writing before proceeding.

2. Payment Terms
A non-refundable deposit of 50% of the total project fee is due before work begins. The remaining 50% is due upon project completion, prior to live site launch or handoff of final files. Invoices unpaid after 14 days will result in a work pause. Invoices unpaid after 30 days accrue interest at 1.5% per month (18% per annum). Client is responsible for any collection costs incurred.

3. Revisions
Each project includes two (2) rounds of revisions per major deliverable. Revisions are reasonable modifications within the original scope — not new features or changes in direction. Additional revision rounds are billed at $95/hour. All revision requests must be submitted through the client portal within 7 calendar days of delivery. Requests after this window may be treated as new work.

4. Timeline & Delays
Project timelines are estimates contingent on timely Client participation. Client-caused delays extending the project by more than 14 calendar days will result in a revised delivery date. Projects inactive for 30+ days due to Client non-response may be subject to a restart fee.

5. Client Responsibilities
Client agrees to provide all required content, assets, brand materials, and login credentials in a timely manner; ensure all content provided is accurate and does not infringe third-party rights; designate one point of contact for approvals; and respond to requests within 5 business days. Studio is not responsible for errors caused by incomplete or inaccurate client content.

6. Intellectual Property
Upon receipt of full and final payment, Client owns all custom design, copy, and code created specifically for this project. Prior to final payment, all work-in-progress remains the property of Studio. Studio retains the right to display completed work in its portfolio unless Client requests otherwise in writing. Studio retains ownership of all pre-existing tools, frameworks, and templates used.

7. Third-Party Platforms
Client's project may rely on third-party platforms including hosting, payment processors, form services, email providers, and analytics tools. Studio is not responsible for downtime, pricing changes, feature removals, data loss, or policy changes by any third party. Additional work due to third-party changes is subject to separate billing.

8. Results Disclaimer
Studio makes no guarantees of specific search engine rankings, traffic levels, lead volumes, or revenue outcomes. SEO and digital marketing results vary based on industry competition, market conditions, content quality, and Google algorithm changes. Any projections shared are illustrative only and do not constitute financial guarantees.

9. Confidentiality
Both parties agree to keep the other's proprietary business information, pricing, and client data confidential. Studio will not share Client logins, business data, or confidential information with third parties except as required to deliver the project. This obligation survives termination.

10. Limitation of Liability
Studio's total liability for any claim arising from this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client for the project. In no event shall Studio be liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including lost profits or business interruption, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

11. Termination & Kill Fee
Either party may terminate with 7 days written notice. If Client terminates after work has begun: (a) all completed work is invoiced at the pro-rata project rate or $95/hour, whichever is greater; (b) the initial deposit is non-refundable; (c) a kill fee equal to 25% of the remaining unbilled balance is due upon termination. Completed deliverables are provided upon payment of all outstanding amounts.

12. Force Majeure
Neither party shall be in default if performance is delayed by circumstances beyond reasonable control, including illness, family emergency, natural disaster, or government action. The affected party shall notify the other within 5 business days.

13. Dispute Resolution
Disputes shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved, disputes shall be settled by binding arbitration in Los Angeles County, California, under AAA rules, unless the claim qualifies for California Small Claims Court, in which case either party may elect that jurisdiction.

14. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. Venue for any proceedings shall be Los Angeles County, California.

15. Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with any project proposal or scope of work, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions. Acknowledgment below constitutes a legally binding electronic signature under the E-SIGN Act and California Civil Code § 1633.1 et seq.
