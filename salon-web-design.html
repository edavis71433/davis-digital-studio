The problem

Why most salon & spa websites lose customers

A weak website doesn't just look bad. It actively costs you leads, bookings, and revenue every single day.

Booking is buried or missing

If a client has to call to book, half of them won't. Online booking integrated into every page, with a prominent 'Book Now' button above the fold.

No gallery to show your work

Clients hire based on results they can see. A properly structured photo gallery with alt text also ranks in Google Images — free traffic.

Not showing up in local search

For every search like 'balayage near me' or 'facial [your city]' that you don't appear in, someone else is getting that booking.

Outdated or generic branding

Your website should feel like walking into your salon. If it's a generic template, it's not building the trust that keeps clients coming back.

What I build

Everything your salon & spabusiness actually needs

Not a template. A site built around how your specific customers find, evaluate, and decide to hire you.

Online booking integration

Vagaro, Square Appointments, Acuity, or direct booking links built into every page. The goal: zero friction between finding you and booking with you.

Photo gallery & portfolio

A mobile-optimized gallery that loads fast, showcases your best work, and is structured for Google Images. Let the results speak for themselves.

Service menu with pricing

Clear services, descriptions, and pricing — structured so Google can read it. Clients who know your prices before calling convert better and waste less of your time.

Local SEO for beauty searches

On-page SEO targeting 'hair salon [city]', 'balayage near me', and your specific services. Google Business Profile setup and review strategy included.

Staff profiles & specialties

Clients often book with a specific stylist. Individual profiles with photos and specialty services help clients find the right person and build loyalty.

Gift cards & retail products

Gift cards are passive revenue — especially around holidays. Retail product sales integrated into the site turn your shelves into an online store.
