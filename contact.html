Common questions

Things people
usually ask.

What's the discovery call actually like?
Thirty minutes, no presentation. I want to understand your business, what's frustrating you about your current online presence, and what success looks like for you. At the end I'll tell you honestly whether I think I can help and what I'd recommend.
How long does a project take?
A full website redesign typically takes 45–60 days from strategy to launch. The timeline depends on the scope and how quickly we can get feedback and content from your end. I'll give you a realistic estimate before we start.
Do I need to provide my own content?
Not necessarily. Copywriting is available as an add-on. If you do have existing content, I'll review it and let you know what needs updating. Most clients do a mix — I write the homepage and key pages, they handle the rest.
What platform do you build on?
It depends on your needs. Squarespace for most small businesses (easy for you to maintain). Webflow for more complex sites. Custom HTML/CSS when something specific is required. I'll recommend the right tool for your situation, not the one that's easiest for me.
What happens after the site launches?
You get a 30-day post-launch check-in included with every project. After that, optional monthly retainers are available for ongoing strategy, content updates, and SEO improvements. Some clients want to maintain things themselves — totally fine too.
