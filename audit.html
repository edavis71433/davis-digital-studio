Things people ask.

Is the free score automated?
Yes — the free Site Score tool pulls real data directly from Google's PageSpeed Insights API and shows you your actual Lighthouse scores. The paid audits are manually reviewed and written by Eric personally.
Is the audit fee credited toward a project?
Yes. Every paid audit fee is credited toward any full project. So if you move forward with a redesign, you've already applied what you paid toward the total.
How is this different from Google PageSpeed?
Google PageSpeed gives you scores with no context. This tool gives you scores plus a plain-English explanation of what each one means for your specific business, ranked by what will actually move the needle.
What if I want to fix things myself?
Completely fine. Every finding and recommendation is written to be actionable whether or not you hire me.
