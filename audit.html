Questions

Things people ask.

Is this just an automated report?
No. I use tools like SEMrush and Google Lighthouse to pull data, but every finding is reviewed and written by me personally. You're getting my analysis, not a generated PDF.
Do I need to do anything to prepare?
Just your website URL and 3 minutes to fill out the intake form. I handle everything else. no access to your accounts or backend required.
What if I want to fix things myself?
That's completely fine. The report is written to be actionable whether or not you hire me. Every recommendation includes enough context to act on it independently.
Is the audit fee credited toward a project?
Every paid audit fee is credited toward any full project. So if you move forward with a redesign, you've already applied what you paid toward the total.
How is this different from free tools like Google PageSpeed?
Free tools give you scores with no context. I give you scores plus an explanation of what they mean for your specific business, ranked by what will actually move the needle for you.
